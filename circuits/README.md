
# Zero-Knowledge Circuits

This directory contains the **Circom circuits** used by the ZK-DarkPool AI prototype to verify private order validity and trade compatibility without exposing sensitive order information.

The circuits allow the system to validate cryptographic properties of orders while keeping private values such as exact prices, volumes, and nonces outside the public proof signals.

## Circuit Overview

| Circuit | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `order_validity.circom` | Verifies that a private order is valid and correctly linked to its cryptographic commitments |
| `match_compatibility.circom` | Verifies that a BUY and SELL order satisfy the required compatibility conditions for matching |

---

# `order_validity.circom`

The `order_validity` circuit proves that a private order satisfies the system's validity constraints.

The circuit verifies that:

- The private `price` correctly corresponds to the provided `price_commitment`.
- The private `volume` correctly corresponds to the provided `volume_commitment`.
- The price is within the configured range:

```text
MIN_PRICE <= price <= MAX_PRICE
```

- The volume is within the configured range:

```text
MIN_VOLUME <= volume <= MAX_VOLUME
```

## Private Witness Inputs

The following values remain private during proof verification:

```text
price
volume
price_nonce
volume_nonce
```

The nonces are used when constructing cryptographic commitments, ensuring that the committed values cannot be directly inferred from the public commitments.

## Public Signals

The following commitment values are exposed as public signals:

```text
price_commitment
volume_commitment
```

A verifier can therefore confirm that the order is valid and correctly committed without receiving the underlying price or volume.

---

# `match_compatibility.circom`

The `match_compatibility` circuit verifies whether two private orders satisfy the conditions required for a valid trade.

For a BUY order and a SELL order, the circuit proves that:

```text
buy_price >= sell_price
```

This confirms that the buyer's maximum acceptable price is sufficient to execute against the seller's minimum acceptable price.

The circuit also verifies:

```text
buy_volume == sell_volume
```

This ensures that the two orders are compatible according to the prototype's current exact-volume matching rule.

## Privacy Properties

During proof generation, the following information remains private:

```text
buy_price
sell_price

buy_volume
sell_volume

buy_price_nonce
sell_price_nonce

buy_volume_nonce
sell_price_nonce
```

The verifier receives the proof and associated public signals required for verification, rather than the underlying private order values.

---

# Privacy Model

The circuits are designed around the following principle:

> **Verify that an order or trade satisfies the required conditions without publicly revealing the private values used to satisfy those conditions.**

In the prototype:

1. A trader creates a private order.
2. Private price and volume values are cryptographically committed.
3. The `order_validity` circuit verifies that the order is valid.
4. Candidate BUY/SELL pairs are generated by the matching pipeline.
5. The AI routing layer prioritizes candidate pairs before expensive ZK processing.
6. The `match_compatibility` circuit verifies whether the selected orders satisfy the trade conditions.
7. Valid matches can proceed to the settlement layer.
